2. Non-Stretch & Cotton Lining Advisory

IMPORTANT FIT NOTE:

All measurements listed are ready-garment dimensions. Every Thunivi garment features 100% breathable cotton lining with zero stretch. For a comfortable, daily-wear fit, choose a garment size that is 1 to 2 inches larger than your natural body measurement.

3. How to Measure Yourself at Home

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest with a soft measuring tape.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline (narrowest part of your torso, above the navel).
  • Hip: Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Shoulder: Measure from the edge of one shoulder bone across your back to the edge of the other.
